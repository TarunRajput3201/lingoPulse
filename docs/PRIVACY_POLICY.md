# Privacy Policy for LingoPulse

**Last Updated:** October 25, 2026  
**Effective Date:** October 25, 2026

LingoPulse ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application and backend services collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you use the LingoPulse mobile application on iOS and Android.

---

### 1. Information We Collect

* **Account Information:** When you create an account, we collect your email address, username, display name, and password (securely hashed via bcrypt).
* **Learning & Progression Data:** To provide our learning experience, we record your target language, learning level, daily goals, completed lessons, exercise attempts, XP points, streak statistics, and leaderboard standings.
* **Audio & Voice Data:** When you participate in optional spoken pronunciation exercises, audio is temporarily processed on device to evaluate pronunciation accuracy. We do not permanently store your raw voice recordings on our servers.
* **Device & Session Credentials:** We use secure on-device storage (`Expo SecureStore` / iOS Keychain / Android Keystore) to store authentication tokens.

---

### 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected exclusively to:
1. Provide, personalize, and improve the language learning curriculum.
2. Synchronize your progress, XP, streaks, and hearts across sessions.
3. Compute weekly leaderboard standings in your league.
4. Send optional daily learning reminders (only with explicit push notification consent).
5. Detect and prevent fraudulent XP submissions.

---

### 3. Data Retention and Account Deletion

* **In-App Account Deletion:** You can delete your account at any time directly inside the mobile application: navigate to **Profile > Settings > Delete Account**.
* **Complete Data Wipe:** Tapping "Delete Forever" immediately and irreversibly deletes your account record, learning progress, attempts history, streak logs, and leaderboard entries from our production database in accordance with Apple App Store Review Guideline 5.1.1(v) and Google Play User Data policies.

---

### 4. Children’s Privacy

LingoPulse does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 without verified parental consent. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided personal information, we immediately delete it.

---

### 5. Third-Party Services

* **Apple App Store & Google Play Store:** For app installation and optional in-app subscription verification.
* **Cloud Hosting:** Hosted in secure tier-1 cloud environments with encrypted database storage at rest and in transit (TLS 1.3).
