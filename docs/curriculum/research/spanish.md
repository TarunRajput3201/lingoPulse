# Language Curriculum Research: Spanish (es)

## 1. Research Sources & Institutional References
- **Instituto Cervantes**: Plan Curricular del Instituto Cervantes (PCIC) — Niveles de referencia para el español (A1–C2).

## 2. Writing System & Orthography
- **Script**: Latin alphabet (27 letters, including *ñ*).
- **Diacritics**: Acute accent (*tilde*: *á, é, í, ó, ú*) used for orthographic stress rules and disambiguation (*si* = if, *sí* = yes; *el* = the, *él* = he). Inverted question and exclamation marks (*¿...?, ¡...!*).
- **Phonetics & Phonology**: Highly shallow/transparent orthography. 5 pure vowels (/a, e, i, o, u/). Consonantal traits include the alveolar trill (*rr* vs *r*), soft *b/v* merger (/β/), and the distinction (*distinción* /θ/) in Spain vs. *seseo* (/s/) in Latin America.

---

## 3. Typology & Grammatical Architecture
- **Word Order**: Flexible **SVO** with frequent pro-drop (subject pronoun omission because verb endings mark person and number).
- **Gender & Number**: Two grammatical genders (Masculine, Feminine) and two numbers (Singular, Plural). Consistent article and adjective agreement (*el libro rojo*, *las casas blancas*).
- **Key Verbal Contrasts**:
  - **Ser vs. Estar**: Permanent/essential traits, identity, origin, time (*ser*) vs. state, condition, mood, physical location (*estar*).
  - **Pretérito Indefinido vs. Pretérito Imperfecto**: Completed past action with definite endpoints (*fui, comí*) vs. ongoing, habitual, or descriptive past without focus on completion (*iba, comía*).
  - **Subjunctive Mood (*subjuntivo*)**: Used systematically for doubt, emotion, volition, hypothesis, and denial (*Quiero que vengas*).
  - **Por vs. Para**: Cause, motivation, duration, exchange (*por*) vs. destination, deadline, recipient, goal (*para*).

---

## 4. Regional Variations
- **Latin American vs. Peninsular Spanish**:
  - 2nd Person Plural: Latin America uses *ustedes* for both formal and informal plural; Spain uses *vosotros/as* for informal and *ustedes* for formal.
  - Pronunciation: *Ceceo/Distinción* (/θ/ for *c/z*) in Central/Northern Spain vs. universal *seseo* in the Americas.
  - Vocabulary: *ordenador* (Spain) vs. *computadora* (LatAm); *coche* vs. *carro/auto*; *móvil* vs. *celular*.

---

## 5. Common Learner Pitfalls
1. **Direct Object Pronouns & Leísmo**: Confusion between *lo/la* and *le*.
2. **False Friends (*falsos amigos*)**: *embarazada* (pregnant, not embarrassed), *actualmente* (currently, not actually), *éxito* (success, not exit).
3. **Prepositional Accusative (*a personal*)**: Forgetting the *a* when a direct object is a specific person (*Veo a María*).
