# Language Curriculum Research: French (fr)

## 1. Research Sources & Institutional References
- **France Éducation international & Ministère de l'Éducation nationale**: Cadre européen commun de référence pour les langues (CECRL).
- **Alliance Française**: Progression pédagogique internationale (A1–C2).
- **DELF / DALF**: Diplôme d'études en langue française & Diplôme approfondi de langue française.
- **Académie française**: Dictionnaire de l'Académie française et normes orthographiques.

---

## 2. Writing System & Orthography
- **Script**: Latin alphabet (26 letters + 5 diacritics: acute *é*, grave *è, à, ù*, circumflex *â, ê, î, ô, û*, trema *ë, ï, ü*, cedilla *ç*, plus ligatures *œ, æ*).
- **Phonetics & Phonology**:
  - Nasal Vowels: Four distinct phonemes (/ɑ̃, ɛ̃, ɔ̃, œ̃/).
  - Silent Final Consonants: Letters like *-s, -t, -d, -x, -p* are typically silent at the end of words (*chat, vous, petit*).
  - Liaison & Enchaînement: Pronouncing otherwise silent final consonants before a vowel-initial word (*les amis* /le.za.mi/).
  - Elision: Systematic vowel contraction (*le ami* -> *l'ami*; *je aime* -> *j'aime*).

---

## 3. Typology & Grammatical Architecture
- **Word Order**: Strict **SVO**. Inversion occurs in formal questions (*Parlez-vous français ?*).
- **Gender & Number**: Masculine and Feminine. Plural marked orthographically with *-s* or *-x*, though often silent in speech (the article marks plurality: *le livre* /lə livʁ/ vs *les livres* /le livʁ/).
- **Verbal Conjugation**:
  - Three conjugation groups: 1st (*-er*), 2nd (*-ir* with *-issant*), 3rd (irregular *-re, -oir, -ir*).
  - Auxiliary verbs in compound tenses: *avoir* vs. *être* (DR MRS VANDERTRAMP motion/state verbs and all reflexive verbs).
  - Past tense distinction: *Passé composé* (punctual, completed events) vs. *Imparfait* (ongoing background description, habits).
  - Participle agreement rules: Agreement with subject when conjugated with *être*, and with preceding direct object when conjugated with *avoir*.
  - Negation: Dual-element structure (*ne ... pas, ne ... plus, ne ... jamais*).

---

## 4. Regional Variations
- **Metropolitan French vs. Canadian French (Québécois)**:
  - Pronunciation differences, vocabulary (*magasiner* vs *faire du shopping*, *fin de semaine* vs *week-end*), and pronoun usage (*tu* vs *on*).
- **African & Swiss/Belgian French**: Switzerland/Belgium number terms (*septante, nonante* for 70, 90).

---

## 5. Common Learner Pitfalls
1. **Pronoun Placement**: Object pronouns precede the conjugated verb (*Je te vois*, not *Je vois toi*).
2. **False Cognates (*faux amis*)**: *actuellement* (currently, not actually), *attendre* (to wait, not to attend), *blessé* (injured, not blessed).
3. **Partitive Articles**: *du, de la, des, de* (*Je mange du pain*, but *Je ne mange pas de pain*).
